# Privacy Policy for Tiny Hands

**Effective date: September 17, 2026**
**Publisher: Youkar**, independent developer. Contact: YoukarC@hotmail.com

> This text accurately describes what the app does. It is not legal advice.

## In one sentence

Tiny Hands is a focus timer that works without an account and without an
internet connection: the app technically does not have permission to
connect to the network, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Everything you
do in it stays on your phone.

## What stays on your device

During a focus session (15, 25, 45, or 60 minutes), a potter cat or a baker
frog crafts a unique piece; once the session ends, that piece joins your
collection.

Only the following is saved, locally, in a database on your device:

- your settings (chosen world/theme, music, reduced animations, keep-screen-on
  option);
- your sessions (the activity name you freely type in, duration, dates,
  status);
- the pieces in your collection (the recipes of the crafted items).

None of this ever leaves your device. Uninstalling Tiny Hands, or clearing
the app's data in Android settings, permanently deletes this information.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. Tiny Hands' installation package does not include the Internet
permission: the app has no technical way of sending any data over a network,
of any kind. There is no network code in the app whatsoever.

## Advertising

No advertising.

## In-app purchases

No in-app purchases.

## Permissions, and why

- **Notifications** — to show a notification while a session is running in
  the background, and a notification when a session ends. This permission is
  requested once, the first time you start a session; if you decline, the
  app keeps working normally, only these notifications won't appear.
- **Boot completed** — to reschedule the end-of-session notification if your
  phone restarts while a session is running. Without this, that notification
  would be lost on restart.
- **Vibrate** — the tactile feedback of notifications.
- **Network state** — this permission is added automatically by a technical
  library used in the app; it is unusable without Internet access, which the
  app does not request. Tiny Hands never reads your network status.
- **Keep screen on** — only if you enable the "Keep screen on" option in
  settings, and only while a session is shown in the foreground.

Tiny Hands does not request access to your location, contacts, camera,
microphone, photo library, or any advertising identifier.

## What the app does not do

There is **no account** to create. There is **no advertising**, **no
in-app purchases**, **no analytics or audience-measurement tool**, and **no
crash-reporting tool**. Nothing is sold, rented, shared, or transmitted to
anyone — not as a promise, but because there is technically no way to do so:
the app cannot connect to the Internet.

## Your backups

In Settings, "Export my workshop" displays your backup as JSON text in a
window, with a "Copy backup" button that places it on the clipboard; you
then decide where to paste and keep it (a note, an email to yourself, a
file). "Restore a backup" opens Android's system file picker to read back a
JSON file you choose (10 MB maximum), and asks you to confirm before it
replaces your current data. Outside of this last action, which you trigger
yourself, the app has no general access to your device's storage.

You decide where what you copy or that file goes; if you choose to place it
in another service, that service's privacy policy applies from that point
on.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time, in three ways:

- in the app, Settings → "Erase all my data";
- by uninstalling the app;
- by clearing the app's data in Android settings.

If you have enabled automatic Android device backup (a Google service, tied
to your phone), Tiny Hands' data may be included in your Google account
backup, depending on your device settings. This is not something the app
controls: it's a mechanism of the operating system, not a service the
publisher operates or controls.

## Children

Tiny Hands is intended for a general audience and does not specifically
target children. The app collects no data, regardless of the age of the
person using it.

## Your rights

GDPR grants you a right of access, rectification, erasure, and data
portability. Here, these rights are exercised directly on your device,
without writing to us and without delay:

- **Access and rectification**: everything is displayed and editable in the
  app.
- **Portability**: the export function hands your data back to you as JSON.
- **Erasure**: "Erase all my data" in settings, or uninstalling the app.

We never receive or hold any copy of your data: we could neither send it to
you nor delete it on your behalf, since we don't have it. You are still
welcome to write to us with any question.
